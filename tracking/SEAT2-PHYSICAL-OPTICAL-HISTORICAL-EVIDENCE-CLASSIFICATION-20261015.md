## Scientific consistency

The computer is real physical matter, electricity, time, temperature and semiconductor memory; a CPU computational proof is evidence of physically executed information processing. OS process metrics *are* measured host behavior. A cloud OS process's RSS value is not automatically a measured photon rate or separate optical retention mechanism. Both statements are consistent.

An operator can have performed a physical photon-memory measurement whose unshared or incomplete instrumentation trail has not yet been independently certified. The correct action is provenance stratification and asking for its source instrument files, not denying the observation. The LIRIS SH pre-light event-order experiment is relevant evidence in Asolaria's history but is not interchangeable with a time-resolved photon-memory write/block/hold/read record.

No quantum causation, beyond-light causal transfer, or independent optical-storage calibration should be inferred from the record label `QUANTUM_BY_DEFINITION` alone; that is documented as `OPERATOR_CANON`.
